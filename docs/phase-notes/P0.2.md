# P0.2 - Trade and logistics write path

## What was built
- **trade:** `POST /api/trades` derives quotas from delivery terms (MONTHLY, WEEKLY, DAILY, CUSTOM), `GET` endpoints, and
  `GET /api/quotas/{ref}` (the endpoint behind the `QuotaDirectory` port).
- **logistics:** `POST /api/quotas/{q}/assignments`, `PATCH /api/assignments/{ref}`, `POST /api/assignments/{ref}/events`,
  `GET /api/quotas/{q}/qag?asOf=`. Each material change cuts a QAG revision and records a diff-carrying event.
- **platform:** transactional outbox (writer, polling publisher, poller lifecycle), Kafka sink with envelope headers,
  problem+json error rendering.
- **contracts:** `QagRevisionEvent`, `ShippingOperationalEvent`, `ChangeKind`, `Topics`, ports `QuotaDirectory` and `BusinessDayClock`.
- **stubs:** in-memory Business Day Control (one BRD per desk, roll forward).
- 51 tests in the build, all passing. 10 of them run Postgres and/or Redpanda in Docker.

## Decisions and why
1. **Outbox, never a direct Kafka send.** Business row + event row commit together, so "state changed" and "event will be published"
   are one atomic fact. A rolled-back change leaves no event (`rolledBackTransactionPublishesNothing`). Delivery is at-least-once,
   so consumers dedup on `eventId`.
2. **Polling outbox, not CDC.** CDC on Oracle needs LogMiner or a GoldenGate licence; polling with `SKIP LOCKED` is slower but licence-free.
   Say the trade-off out loud: added latency of one poll interval, and per-key order only guaranteed with a single publisher.
3. **Publish the diff.** Logistics knows what changed, so the event says so (`assignmentsAdded/Removed/Modified` plus full `members`).
   Legacy consumers each diffed old against new and could disagree.
4. **Producer classifies material vs operational.** The caller supplies a `changeKind`; only material kinds cut a revision. Operational
   events go to a separate high-volume topic, so revision consumers never pay for them.
5. **Per-quota advisory lock.** The revision chain, the ref sequence and the quota cap are all read-decide-write over one quota.
   `pg_advisory_xact_lock` serialises writers of one quota only; the concurrency test shows a linear chain and distinct refs under
   10 simultaneous writers. Alternative was optimistic retry: more code, same result at this write rate.
6. **Ports for cross-service reads.** Logistics needs a quota's quantity and desk, but may not touch trade's classes or tables. It uses
   `QuotaDirectory` (HTTP adapter in production, fake in tests) and `BusinessDayClock`.
7. **BRD, not wall clock**, stamped on every revision and event from the desk's business date.
8. **Errors carry the numbers** (`quotaQty`, `existingAssignedQty`, `requestedQty`), rendered as problem+json.
9. **No blanket 400 for `IllegalArgumentException`.** It hid a real server bug (missing `-parameters` flag) as a client error. Client
   mistakes are raised explicitly; everything else is a 500.

## Likely follow-up questions
- *What if the broker is down?* Business writes still succeed and rows wait in the outbox (`failedDeliveryStaysUnsentAndIsRetriedInOrder`).
- *What if the publisher crashes after sending but before marking sent?* The row is sent again: at-least-once, hence dedup on `eventId`.
- *Why not key Kafka by dealId?* Energy desk-default deals would put a whole desk on one partition. Keys are quotaRef / assignmentRef.
- *How do you keep the revision count near ten per trade?* Only material changes cut one; operational events use their own topic.
- *What if two people edit one quota at once?* They queue on the quota lock; different quotas run in parallel.
- *Why is approval not here?* Pricing owns it; it gates valuation and P&L eligibility and does not cut a QAG revision.

## Not built (on purpose)
Consumers (pricing arrives in P0.3), consumer dedup table and DLQ (P0.3), gateway (P0.4), the app module that runs all services in one JVM
(P0.5), splits and merges as first-class operations (`SPLIT`, `MERGE` are accepted as change kinds on PATCH/POST only).

## Assumptions to confirm (see `docs/SPEC-ADDENDUM.md`)
Monthly clipping, 7-day weekly blocks, only ACTIVE assignments modifiable, no-op PATCH cuts nothing, refs never reused.

## Environment gotchas found
- The build needs `-parameters` (the Boot Gradle plugin adds it; we do not use that plugin).
- Redpanda image is pinned to `v23.3.10` in the outbox test for reproducibility.
